Nautiloid (Mega Drive) in the browser

The game ROM (nautiloid.bin) is built with SGDK (MIT license, https://github.com/Stephane-D/SGDK).
A fan project inspired by Baldur's Gate 3 and Dungeons & Dragons; not affiliated with Larian
Studios or Wizards of the Coast. All graphics, music and sounds are made for this project.

The player in data/ is EmulatorJS (GPL-3.0, https://github.com/EmulatorJS/EmulatorJS) with the
Genesis Plus GX core (Charles MacDonald / Eke-Eke, non-commercial license,
https://github.com/ekeeke/Genesis-Plus-GX). Both are unchanged copies from https://cdn.emulatorjs.org/stable/.
Because of the Genesis Plus GX license the game must be published free of charge.
